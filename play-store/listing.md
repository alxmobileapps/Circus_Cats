## Full description (max 4000)
Step right up to the greatest show under the big blue tent! 🎪🐱

In Circus Cats your cat runs across the circus ring all by itself. Your job is simple: tap to jump through the rings of fire. Easy to learn, hard to put down!

🔥 HOW TO PLAY
• Quick tap = small hop
• Hold = big leap
• Jump cleanly through every ring of fire
• Touch the flames, jump over the ring or bump into its stand, and your cat cries. Try again!

🎪 THE SHOW GETS HARDER
• Rings come in different sizes, heights and spacing, and get smaller as you score
• Two low rings close together need two quick taps
• Twin rings: one jump through both for +2
• Rolling circus balls to jump over
• Rings that glide up and down
• Two bouncing circus balls at once for the real stars

🐾 COLLECT 50 CATS
Every ring you pass gives you 1 coin. Spend coins in the Cat Shop to unlock new cats:
• 30 house cats: Maine Coon, Persian, Siamese, Ragdoll, British Shorthair, Bengal, Scottish Fold, Sphynx, Russian Blue, Norwegian Forest Cat, Abyssinian, Devon Rex, Bombay and many more
• 20 Big & Wild Cats: Tiger, Lion, Leopard, Cheetah, Jaguar, Snow Leopard, Black Panther, Cougar, Caracal, Serval, Lynx, Ocelot, Pallas's Cat, Sand Cat and more

Every cat has its own look and its own voice: a meow, a "mrrp!" chirp, a snarl, or a mighty ROAR when it jumps.

✨ FEATURES
• One-tap controls, perfect for short breaks
• Cheerful circus march music
• Beat your best score and share it with friends
• Works offline, no account needed
• Family friendly, for all ages

Can you get all 50 cats? Tap to start the show!
